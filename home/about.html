about me

I'm Ren, a web designer and digital nonamd based in Seattle, Washington. I'm currently studying Visual Communication Design at U-Dub.

I work with a variety of mediums, from UX/UI design to social media and brand identity. I am versatile and adaptive, being able to learn new skills on the go to meet my team's needs.

In my free time you can catch me spending time in nature, trying new recipes, and listening to shoegaze. I enjoy indie games like Hades 2 and Hollow Knight Silksong.

If you have any questions or think we'd be a good fit, feel free to contact me. I'd love the opportunity to work together!
